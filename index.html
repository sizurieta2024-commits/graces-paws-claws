How booking works

Four easy steps

  1. Message me

    Send me a message on Facebook Messenger with your dates and a little about your pets.

About me

Good to know

Questions, answered

What areas do you cover?

Magnolia, Texas and nearby areas like The Woodlands, Spring, Conroe and Tomball. Message me your area and I'll let you know.

Can you watch my pets at your house?

No. I'm not able to bring pets to my own home, so all drop-ins and overnights happen at your house, where your pets are most comfortable.

How does pricing work?

$25 per cat, $50 per dog and $75 per horse, per day. Drop-ins and overnights are the same rate. A flat $50 travel/gas fee is added once to your final total, and it never changes.

Can I book by phone or email?

No. Because of scammers, I only take bookings through Facebook Messenger. Just send me a message there.

Do we need to meet first?

Yes, always. I meet every pet and owner in person, as soon as possible, before accepting any payment or scheduling any services.

How do I pay?

Zelle or cash only.

Can you take my dog to their activities?

Yes. If your dog is signed up for training, daycare or another activity, I can bring them there while you're away.

Going out of town soon?

Send me your dates and tell me about your pets. We'll set up a meet & greet and get you booked.
